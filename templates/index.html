About Us

Two restaurants. One passion. Authentic Portugal.

Located on the historic Rua & Largo José Afonso in Setúbal, our two restaurants sit as neighbours — each a celebration of a different facet of Portuguese cuisine.

Original's Casa de Peixe brings you the freshest catch from the Atlantic — sea bream, cod, octopus, prawns — cooked with recipes passed down through generations of coastal cooks.

Lapicanha is our premium grill house. We specialize in perfectly prepared picanha and other prime cuts, paired with authentic Portuguese sides, wines, and a warm, rustic atmosphere.
